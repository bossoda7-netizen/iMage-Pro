# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

This policy describes the current public preview of iMage Pro for macOS.

## What the app processes

iMage Pro processes images, projects, text, selections, and editing settings on
the user's Mac. The Smart Repair feature runs its bundled model locally. The
current preview does not require an account and does not automatically upload
image data or project files to a server.

The app stores local preferences and project-library metadata in macOS app
storage, such as language, appearance, recent project locations, tool defaults,
and recovery information. These values remain on the Mac and are used to
restore the app's local state.

## What the app collects

The current preview does not include advertising identifiers, analytics, cross-app
tracking, or a remote account system. It does not sell or share image content.
When the user chooses an Open, Save, Export, or Show in Finder action, macOS
may present the normal file access UI and the app accesses only the files the
user selects or authorizes.

## Third-party components

The app includes local open-source components and model files. They run as part
of the app; they do not create an account or send project content to their
authors. Their licenses and notices are listed in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and
in the app's Third-Party Licenses panel.

## Links and voluntary support

GitHub and support links in About open the user’s browser only when selected.
No third-party button image or tracking script is loaded inside the app.
GitHub, Ko-fi and payment services process visits and voluntary support under
their own policies; payment details are not sent to iMage Pro.

## Changes

If a future release adds cloud processing, accounts, analytics, crash reporting,
or other data collection, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will
be updated before that feature is released.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact bossoda7@gmail.com. Messages are processed by
the email provider and used to respond to your request. Do not include private
images or project files in a public issue. Website visits and voluntary exhibition
submissions are covered by the website's separate privacy notice.
